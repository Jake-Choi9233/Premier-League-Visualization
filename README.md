# ⚽ Premier League 2024-25: Bargain Star Hunter

> **TL;DR**: Stop overpaying for overrated players. Find hidden gems in the Premier League using data, not hype.

**Live Demo** 👉 [jake-choi9233.github.io/Premier-League-Visualization](https://jake-choi9233.github.io/Premier-League-Visualization/soccer/dashboard/dashboard.html)

***

## 🤔 What's This About?

Ever wondered why some clubs spend £100M on a flop while others unearth world-class talent for peanuts? 

This project turns cold, hard stats into **visual stories** that reveal:
- 💎 Which players punch above their wage grade
- 💸 Who's getting paid like Messi but playing like... well, not Messi
- ⚖️ Where the sweet spot between performance and price actually lives

Think of it as **Moneyball meets FIFA Ultimate Team** — except it's real life, and the data doesn't lie.

***

## 🎮 Features That Actually Matter

### 📍 **Interactive Dashboard**
Drop players onto a virtual pitch like you're managing your own squad. Click any name to see their stats explode into radar charts and heat maps.

**Why it's cool**: No more scrolling through endless spreadsheets. Just point, click, and *wow*.

***

### 🌐 **3D Sphere / Sunburst Graph**
Watch how each player's contribution pulls the team in different directions — like gravity, but for football.

**The magic**: Instantly spot if your favorite club relies too heavily on one superstar (spoiler: they probably do).

***

### 💰 **Wage Breakdown**
Compare salaries by:
- Team (guess which London club tops the chart 👀)
- Position (spoiler: goalkeepers are underpaid)
- Country (Premier League = global marketplace)

**The tea**: Find out who's robbing their club blind and who deserves a raise.

***

### 📊 **Cost-Effectiveness Analyzer**
The MVP feature. Sort players by **value per pound spent** and discover:
- ✅ **Steals**: Elite output, mid-table wages
- ❌ **Busts**: Champagne salary, lemonade stats

**Real talk**: This is how small clubs compete with oil money.
